This is the audit entry point for the two main theorems of the paper,
Theorem 1.1 (`thm:structure`) and Theorem 1.2 (`thm:real`). The clauses are
those of the tables "Main theorems, clause by clause" in
[FORMALIZATION.md](FORMALIZATION.md). For each clause it gives the manuscript
statement, the Lean declarations with their complete types, the hypotheses and
how they differ from the paper, the axioms and a status.

## Theorem 1.1 (`thm:structure`)

### S1. N(G) = Q(G)

**Manuscript.** `thm:structure`, proved as `thm:degree` (Theorem 2.5): for
every finite loopless multigraph, the generic number N(G) of admissible complex
BP solutions equals the state sum Q(G) = Σ_S wt(S).

**Lean.** `Paper.thm_structure_N_eq_Q`; `Paper.thm_degree` (the generic degree
is unique); `Paper.prop_bp_family_finite_etale` (algebraic form).

<!-- lean-check: BPFixedPoints.Paper.thm_structure_N_eq_Q BPFixedPoints.Paper.thm_degree BPFixedPoints.Paper.prop_bp_family_finite_etale -->
```lean

  [inst_1 : DecidableEq E] [inst_2 : Fintype V] [inst_3 : Fintype E] (G : BPFixedPoints.Multigraph V E),
  G.Loopless → G.HasGenericDegree G.Q

@BPFixedPoints.Paper.thm_degree : ∀ {V : Type u_1} {E : Type u_2} [inst : DecidableEq V] [inst_1 : DecidableEq E]
  [inst_2 : Fintype V] [inst_3 : Fintype E] (G : BPFixedPoints.Multigraph V E),
  G.Loopless → ∀ {n : ℕ}, G.HasGenericDegree n ↔ n = G.Q

@BPFixedPoints.Paper.prop_bp_family_finite_etale : ∀ {V E : Type} [inst : DecidableEq V] [inst_1 : DecidableEq E]
  [inst_2 : Fintype V] [inst_3 : Fintype E] (G : BPFixedPoints.Multigraph V E),
  G.Loopless →
    ∃ s,
      s ≠ 0 ∧
        ∏ j, MvPolynomial.X j ∣ s ∧
          Module.Finite (Localization.Away s) (G.FamilyAway s) ∧
            Module.Free (Localization.Away s) (G.FamilyAway s) ∧
              Module.finrank (Localization.Away s) (G.FamilyAway s) = G.Q ∧
                Algebra.Etale (Localization.Away s) (G.FamilyAway s) ∧
                  ∀ (a : E → ℂ) (b : V → ℂ),
                    (MvPolynomial.eval (Sum.elim a b)) s ≠ 0 →
                      Nat.card { y // G.IsAdmissibleSolution a b y } = G.Q ∧
                        ∀ (y : BPFixedPoints.BP.Dart E → ℂ), G.IsAdmissibleSolution a b y → G.IsSimpleSolution a b y
```

**Hypotheses.** Finite V and E; `G.Loopless`. These are the paper's.

**Differences.** N(G) = n is the counting statement `G.HasGenericDegree n`:
there is a nonzero P ∈ ℤ[a, b] such that at every complex (a, b) with all
a_e, b_i nonzero and P(a, b) ≠ 0 there are exactly n admissible solutions, all
simple. Admissible (`IsAdmissibleSolution`) is the polynomial fixed-point
system with nonzero messages, update denominators and vertex factors
(`eq:admissibility`); simple (`IsSimpleSolution`) is a nonzero Jacobian
determinant. The paper defines N(G) as the degree of the admissible family,
finite étale over a nonempty Zariski-open set. `prop_bp_family_finite_etale`
gives this form for the ring map: for some s ≠ 0 divisible by the product of
the coordinates, the localized family ring `G.FamilyAway s` is finite, free of
rank Q(G) and étale; s is not claimed to cut out the largest étale locus.
Route: Lemma 2.3 is proved in valuation form for any valued field, and the
constancy of the generic fiber uses shape-lemma certificates instead of generic
finite étaleness (FORMALIZATION.md, "Differences in hypotheses").

### S2. 𝒰_G is a normal domain

**Manuscript.** `thm:structure`, `alg:normal-cm` (Proposition 3.6):
𝒰_G = Γ(Z_G, 𝒪_{Z_G}) is a normal domain.

**Lean.** `Paper.alg_normal_domain`.

<!-- lean-check: BPFixedPoints.Paper.alg_normal_domain -->
```lean
@BPFixedPoints.Paper.alg_normal_domain : ∀ {V E : Type} [inst : DecidableEq V] [inst_1 : Fintype V]
  [inst_2 : DecidableEq E] [inst_3 : Fintype E] (G : BPFixedPoints.Multigraph V E),
  IsDomain ↥G.IncidenceRing ∧ IsIntegrallyClosed ↥G.IncidenceRing
```

**Hypotheses.** Finite V and E. No looplessness assumption.

**Differences.** 𝒰_G is the chart-intersection model `G.IncidenceRing`; its
identification with the global sections of the projective zero scheme is printed
under [Lean objects](#lean-objects). Normal domain is Mathlib's `IsDomain` and
`IsIntegrallyClosed`. Route: the manuscript deduces both properties from the
smoothness and irreducibility of the vector bundle Z_G → X_G; Lean uses the
chart form of this argument: every chart ring is a polynomial ring, the
intersection lies in the common torus ring, and the intersection is integrally
closed (FORMALIZATION.md, "How the main results are proved"). Z_G is
formalized as the total space of the rank-3m kernel bundle
(`Paper.alg_vector_bundle_total_space`), but the normality argument through
its smoothness and irreducibility is not.

**Status.** (a).

### S3. 𝒰_G is Cohen–Macaulay

**Manuscript.** `thm:structure`, `alg:normal-cm`: 𝒰_G is Cohen–Macaulay. The
proof gets depth = dimension at the homogeneous maximal ideal from the
resolution and passes to every prime; `supp:cm-localization` localizes the
resolution at every prime.

**Lean.** `Paper.alg_cohenMacaulay` (maximal ideals),
`Paper.alg_cohenMacaulay_all_primes` (all primes).

    ∀ (P : Ideal ↥G.IncidenceRing) [inst_4 : P.IsMaximal],

      ↑(IsLocalRing.depth ↧(Localization.AtPrime P)) = ringKrullDim (Localization.AtPrime P)

**Hypotheses.** Finite V and E, an edge order. No looplessness assumption.

**Differences.** Cohen–Macaulay is stated as: 𝒰_G is Noetherian, and at every
prime (resp. maximal ideal) P the localization has depth equal to its Krull
dimension; depth is Mathlib's `IsLocalRing.depth` (Ext depth) of the
localization as a module over itself. Route: Lean passes maximal-length regular
sequences from polynomial rings to the finite free parameter algebra, instead
of localizing the resolution (docstring of `Paper.alg_cohenMacaulay_all_primes`).

**Status.** (b). The localization argument of `supp:cm-localization` is
formalized in its module form: for every prime 𝔭 ⊇ I of S_G,

statement above is not formalized; the ring statement is proved by another
argument (FORMALIZATION.md, "How the main results are proved").

### S4. 𝒰_G is finite over S_G

**Manuscript.** `thm:structure`, `alg:incidence-vector-bundle` (Lemma 3.3),
proved in `supp:vector-bundle`: Z_G is closed in X_G × 𝔹_G and X_G is
projective, so π_G is proper, (π_G)_* 𝒪 is coherent and 𝒰_G is a finite
S_G-module.

**Lean.** `Multigraph.incidenceRing_finite`.

<!-- lean-check: BPFixedPoints.Multigraph.incidenceRing_finite -->
```lean

  [inst_2 : DecidableEq E] [inst_3 : Fintype E] (G : BPFixedPoints.Multigraph V E), G.coeffHom.Finite
```

**Hypotheses.** Finite V and E. No looplessness assumption.

**Differences.** `G.coeffHom.Finite`: the structure map S_G → 𝒰_G is finite,
that is, 𝒰_G is a finitely generated S_G-module. Route: Lean proves it by
integrality over every valuation ring containing the coefficients and the
finiteness of the integral closure (FORMALIZATION.md, "How the main results are
proved"), not through properness. Properness of the projective zero scheme over
the coefficient space is a separate theorem
(`Paper.alg_incidence_projective_proper`, [Lean objects](#lean-objects)).

**Status.** (b). The manuscript's argument (coherence of the direct image under
the proper map π_G) is not the one formalized; properness of π_G is proved,
the coherence of the direct image is not.

### S5. dim 𝒰_G = d_G = 3m + n − τ

**Manuscript.** `thm:structure`, `alg:normal-cm`: dim 𝒰_G = d_G. The proof
bounds the dimension above by the dimensions 3m_C + n_C of the vector bundles
Z_C and below by the depth from the resolution.

**Lean.** `Multigraph.ringKrullDim_incidenceRing`; `Paper.thm_structure_dim`
(d_G + ρ = 4m).

### S6. Minimal graded free resolution and Betti numbers
